How to book your day

We're so glad you're thinking of Flint Hall for your wedding. Booking is simple, and you don't need a computer for any of it.

1  ·  Fill in your details

Complete Your Details (next page) so we know who you are and when you're dreaming of.

2  ·  Choose your package & catering

Pick the package that suits, and tick your feasting choices on the catering sheet. Nothing is final — we can adjust it together.

3  ·  Read & sign the agreement

Read the venue-hire agreement at the back and sign it. There's nothing unusual in it, but it's important you know what's included.

4  ·  Pay your deposit

Pay the 50% deposit and the £250 refundable damage deposit by bank transfer (details near the back). Your date is only held once both the signed agreement and deposit are with us.

5  ·  Return this pack

Post it to us or hand it in on a visit — see the last page for where to send it.

Questions at any point? Call or email us — we'd far rather talk it through than have you wondering. info@flinthall.uk · 01449 888004 · 07355 364401

How payment works

  • Deposit — 50%, non-refundable, due on booking. This secures your date and contract; nothing is held without it.
  • Balance — 50%, due no later than 30 days before your wedding.
  • Damage deposit — £250, refundable, due 30 days before. Returned within 10 days after the day, subject to the usual inspection.
  • Payments are by bank transfer only (details near the back). Late payments incur £10/day until settled.
