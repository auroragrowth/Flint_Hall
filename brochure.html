Welcome
Somewhere that feels like yours
✦

Wood-fired pizzas. Hog roast under canvas. Fires that go past midnight. Bell tents in the meadow and a barn that holds the dancing — Flint Hall is a rustic outdoor venue tucked into acres of Suffolk countryside, built for the moments that matter and run by people who care that they go right.

We host weddings, corporate retreats, proms, team days, birthdays, mini festivals and celebrations of every kind — with the whole place to yourselves: the Ranch, the Barn, the meadow, the padel court and the on-site accommodation.

A heritage timber-framed barn — recognised as being of historic interest — for ceremonies, dining and dancing. Star-lit, beamed, and yours for the day.

Music & dancing

Live bands and DJs are warmly welcome — it's your party. We simply ask that all live and amplified music works within our strict 90 dB sound limit, with a firm 10:45pm music curfew. It's a condition of our licence and non-negotiable, so it's worth briefing your act on both early — any act that can't perform within these limits isn't right for the venue.
