Where to begin

Five decisions that shape everything else

Settle these first — every other choice becomes easier once they’re made.

❦
1
Your budget
Agree a total and who’s contributing. It quietly decides guest numbers, venue and everything after.
2
A rough guest count
Even a range — 60, 100, 150 — shapes which venues and dates work for you.
3
The feeling
Intimate and candlelit, or a big barn party? Pick a mood before you pick details.
4
Season & flexibility
Knowing your season — and whether a weekday works — opens up dates and value.
5
The venue
Book it first. Your date isn’t real until the venue is, and the best dates go early.
